How it works

Three passes, and none of them ask you to open a page builder.

01

Brief

A paragraph about what you sell and who buys it. Attach a logo, a price list or an old site if you have one.

02

Build

The factory chooses a sitemap, writes the copy, sets type and spacing, and wires forms, bookings or checkout as needed.

03

Ship

Review side by side, edit any block in place, then publish to your domain. Rebuild from a new brief whenever the business changes.

What you get

A real site, not a screenshot of one.

Source you own

Static HTML and CSS, exportable to a repository. No runtime lock-in, no proprietary block format.

Fast by construction

Images sized and compressed at build time, fonts subset, scripts kept to what the page actually uses.

Findable

Metadata, schema markup, sitemap and redirects generated from the same brief that wrote the copy.

Rebuildable

Change a line of the brief and rebuild. Edits you made by hand are kept and merged into the new version.
