## 1. Who We Are and Scope

YYZ Caregivers is an Ontario sole proprietorship providing non-clinical private-home personal support services in Toronto (east end / Scarborough), North York, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, and Oshawa. These Terms govern the relationship between YYZ Caregivers and clients who engage these services, and use of the website.

## 2. Nature of Services

Services offered: senior companionship, meal preparation, light housekeeping, mobility assistance, appointment transportation, and medication reminders. All services are non-clinical.

Services explicitly **not** provided: any clinical medical care, nursing tasks, wound care, or administration of injections. Clients requiring these services should seek a licensed clinical provider.

## 3. No Emergency or Medical Use

YYZ Caregivers does not provide emergency response, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis intervention. In an emergency, contact local emergency services (911) directly rather than YYZ Caregivers.

## 4. Screening and Insurance

YYZ Caregivers maintains active general liability insurance. Caregivers are screened through a vulnerable sector police check, a standard criminal record check, reference checks, and verification of PSW certification/credentials.

This section must describe only what is actually true and current at the time of publication, and should be reviewed periodically to confirm it still is.

New clients begin with a free in-home assessment visit. **[OPEN — see Open Items.]** It has not yet been decided whether a separate written service agreement (covering fees, schedule, and scope for that specific client) will be signed in addition to these Terms, or whether these Terms are the only governing document. ## 6. Scheduling and Cancellation

Clients are asked to give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a visit. No penalty or fee applies for cancellations, including those made with less notice; the 48-hour window is a courtesy expectation, not an enforced requirement. **[OPEN]** If a penalty for short-notice cancellation is wanted later, that is a separate decision requiring an update to this section.

## 7. Fees and Payment

Pricing is provided on request and is not published on the website (see `PROJECT-BRIEF.md`). The initial in-home assessment visit is free. Once services are rendered, the intended payment process is: invoice the client after the visit, payment due within 5 days of the invoice date, payable by e-transfer or cheque. No online payment processor is currently used.

## 8. Liability, Warranties, and Indemnification

**Intentionally not drafted.** This section requires language written or reviewed by a lawyer, not generated here. YYZ Caregivers serves seniors and adults needing daily-living support — a population where poorly drafted liability language can either fail to protect the business or unfairly limit a vulnerable client's rights. Do not substitute placeholder or generic boilerplate for this section; have it drafted specifically for this business by a qualified professional before publication.

## 9. Complaints Process

Complaints may be directed to the founder by phone or email using the contact details in Section 14. YYZ Caregivers will respond within 3 business days. **[OPEN]** A formal escalation path beyond direct founder response has not yet been defined.

## 10. Termination of Services

Either the client or YYZ Caregivers may end the service relationship with reasonable notice, without needing to state a specific cause. "Reasonable notice" is intentionally left undefined as a fixed number of days, to be judged based on the circumstances at the time.
