## 1. Who We Are and Scope

YYZ Caregivers is an Ontario sole proprietorship providing non-clinical private-home personal support services in Toronto (east end / Scarborough), North York, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, and Oshawa. These Terms govern the relationship between YYZ Caregivers and clients who engage these services, and use of the website.

## 2. Nature of Services

Services offered: senior companionship, meal preparation, light housekeeping, mobility assistance, appointment transportation, and medication reminders. All services are non-clinical.

Services explicitly **not** provided: any clinical medical care, nursing tasks, wound care, or administration of injections. Clients requiring these services should seek a licensed clinical provider.

## 3. No Emergency or Medical Use

YYZ Caregivers does not provide emergency response, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis intervention. In an emergency, contact local emergency services (911) directly rather than YYZ Caregivers.

## 4. Screening and Insurance

YYZ Caregivers maintains active general liability insurance. Caregivers are screened through a vulnerable sector police check, a standard criminal record check, reference checks, and verification of PSW certification/credentials.
