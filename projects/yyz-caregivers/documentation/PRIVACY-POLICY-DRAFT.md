- **Status:** DRAFT — not legal advice, not approved for publication
- **Drafted:** September 13, 2026
- **Scope:** Describes intended practices for a *future* live inquiry/consultation system. It does not describe the current website, which collects, sends, and stores nothing (see `src/privacy.html` for the accurate current-state pilot policy).

This draft must not be published or represented as YYZ Caregivers' privacy policy until:

## 2. Scope

This policy applies once a live consultation-request or Support Needs inquiry system exists. It covers information submitted through that system and information gathered during an in-home assessment visit or follow-up communication. It does not cover information YYZ Caregivers has not yet decided to collect, and it must not be used to justify collecting more than is described here.

## 3. Information We Collect

When a real inquiry system is live, YYZ Caregivers intends to collect only what is needed to respond to an inquiry and arrange an in-home assessment:

- Name and preferred contact method
- Phone number and/or email address
- General municipality/location (for service-area confirmation)
- A general description of the support needs being sought, in the client's own words

YYZ Caregivers does not collect, and will not ask visitors to submit through this website: diagnoses, medications, treatment details, health card numbers, government identification, financial account information, or emergency contact details. Any such information is exchanged only in person or by phone, once a client relationship is established, consistent with the non-clinical scope of services in `PROJECT-BRIEF.md`.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used only to:

- Respond to the inquiry
- Schedule and prepare for an in-home assessment visit
- Maintain a record of the client relationship, for the retention period described below

Information is not used for marketing to third parties, sold, or shared except as described in Section 6.

## 5. Who Has Access

YYZ Caregivers currently operates as a solo founder/operator. The founder is the only person with access to client inquiry information. If the business grows to include additional staff or contractors, this policy will be updated to describe their access before that access is granted.

## 6. Third-Party Services and Vendors

Inquiries are received and managed by email only — no CRM, scheduling tool, or third-party form processor is used. Client information is not shared with, or processed by, any vendor beyond the email service itself.

## 7. Retention

Client information is retained for a defined period after the engagement ends or an inquiry does not proceed, rather than deleted immediately. **The specific retention period is not yet set — it is pending legal and accounting input** (for example, on any minimum record-keeping requirements that may apply to a sole proprietorship in Ontario) and will be added here before publication.

## 8. Client Rights

Clients and prospective clients may request to:

- Access the information YYZ Caregivers holds about them
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of their information, subject to any minimum retention period described in Section 7

Requests can be made to the privacy contact in Section 10.

## 9. Security Measures

Client inquiries are received through a single email account, accessed only by the founder. **Two-factor authentication is not currently enabled on this account.** Before this system handles real client information, enabling two-factor authentication is strongly recommended as a baseline safeguard against unauthorized access. This section will be updated to reflect the actual security measures in place at the time of publication — it must not describe protections that are not actually active.

## 10. Incident Response

If a data incident occurs (for example, a lost device or unauthorized access to client information), YYZ Caregivers will notify affected clients directly and assess the situation promptly. **The detailed incident response process (assessment steps, notification timeline, and any regulatory notification obligations) is not yet defined and requires legal input** before this section can be considered complete.

## 11. Privacy Contact

Privacy questions and requests under Section 8 should be directed to privacy@getcarequick.ca once the domain is registered and that address is active. Until then, direct them to frontline.mlvn@gmail.com.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

This policy will be reviewed and updated whenever the underlying data practices change — including when a live inquiry system is first launched, when a new vendor is added, or when the retention period or incident response process is finalized. The version date at the top of the published policy will reflect the most recent substantive change.
