### Intake-Form Change Request

The owner initially requested a comprehensive intake form with condition selection and future care-plan recommendations, then approved the safer default: a non-sending Support Needs Assessment using broad daily-living topics. It will produce only a temporary on-screen summary and must not request diagnoses, medication, treatment, health-card, financial, emergency, or detailed care-history information.

- Use “client,” “support needs,” “topics to discuss,” and “summary”; do not imply patient status, clinical intake, diagnosis, triage, eligibility, or a care plan.
- The prototype must have no backend, form destination, email payload, browser storage, cookies, analytics, account, database, scoring, automated recommendation, download, or third-party integration.
- A future live submission system remains separate scope and requires approved privacy status, professional responsibility, minimum necessary fields, consent, data recipients, hosting, vendors, access, retention, deletion, backups, incident response, and privacy/security/legal review.
