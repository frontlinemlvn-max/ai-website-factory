Frequently asked questions

Clear answers, including what is not yet confirmed

Answers are for pilot review

These answers do not confirm live services, prices, availability, qualifications, service boundaries, or response times. They help shape what a future public website should explain.

Service and fit

Is YYZ Caregivers currently offering services through this website?

No. This is a local pilot. It does not accept bookings, arrange services, create a care relationship, or represent confirmed business operations.

What kinds of support may be considered?

The pilot presents personal routines, companionship, meal and light household support, and mobility-related assistance as possible non-clinical categories. Every task, exclusion, and worker-scope detail must be confirmed before launch.

Does the pilot include nursing or medical care?

No clinical service is defined here. The pilot does not promise nursing, medical advice, medication management, therapy, emergency monitoring, or work outside an assigned worker's confirmed training and permitted scope.

How would support be matched to a person's routine?

A future consultation could discuss general preferences, proposed scope, and possible fit. The actual assessment, matching, supervision, and service-planning process has not been approved.

Consultation

What happens when I use the consultation form?

The local demonstration validates the fields in your browser, shows a preview confirmation, and clears the values. It does not send, store, email, book, or create a request.

What information should I share?

Use general, non-sensitive information only. Do not enter health, medication, identification, financial, street-address, or emergency details.

How quickly would someone respond?

For a real email inquiry, the expected response time is within 12 business hours during the preferred contact hours of Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time. The demonstration forms do not send anything, so they cannot receive a response.

Service area

Which communities may YYZ Caregivers serve?

East Toronto and Durham Region are the intended initial service areas. Exact municipalities, postal codes, travel boundaries, and availability at a particular address must be confirmed directly.

Does the listed service area guarantee coverage at my address?

No. The listed areas describe the intended initial market and do not guarantee service at a particular address.

Pricing and scheduling

What does support cost?

Prices, minimum visit lengths, payment terms, and possible funding or insurance arrangements have not been confirmed. No price should be inferred from this pilot.

What hours or availability are offered?

Operating hours, scheduling, cancellations, staffing, and availability are owner decisions still awaiting confirmation. The website does not offer live scheduling.

What qualifications or screening apply?

No qualification, training, screening, supervision, licensing, or insurance claim is approved for publication. The owner must provide evidence and approved wording before launch.

Privacy

Does the form send or store my information?

No. The pilot uses only temporary values on the current page. It makes no submission request, uses no database or browser storage, and clears the form after a successful preview.

Does the pilot use analytics or marketing cookies?

No. This build includes no analytics, marketing cookies, trackers, chat widgets, maps, remote fonts, or third-party form service.

Emergencies

Can I use this website for urgent help?

No. This website is not monitored for emergencies and does not provide emergency response. Seek appropriate local emergency assistance when immediate help is needed.

Owner verification is required

Service operations, care boundaries, qualifications, screening, insurance, pricing, scheduling, cancellations, availability, contacts, privacy terms, and production processes all need direct owner approval.
