Clear answers about our services and this website

This page separates YYZ Caregivers' actual services from how this preview website behaves.

About this website

This website previews the consultation experience only. Contact YYZ Caregivers directly by phone or email for a real response — the forms on this site do not send or store anything.

Service and fit

Can I book a service directly through this website?

Not yet. This website's forms are a preview only — they do not accept bookings or create a service relationship. Contact YYZ Caregivers directly by phone or email to get started.

What kinds of support does YYZ Caregivers offer?

Personal routines (including medication reminders), companionship, meal preparation and light housekeeping, and mobility-related help including appointment transportation. All services are non-clinical.

Does YYZ Caregivers include nursing or medical care?

No. YYZ Caregivers does not provide clinical medical care, nursing tasks, wound care, or administration of injections. Clients who need these services should seek a licensed clinical provider.

How would support be matched to a person's routine?

New clients begin with a free in-home assessment visit to discuss needs and confirm which services fit before service begins.

Consultation

What happens when I use the consultation form?

The local demonstration validates the fields in your browser, shows a preview confirmation, and clears the values. It does not send, store, email, book, or create a request.

What information should I share?

Use general, non-sensitive information only. Do not enter health, medication, identification, financial, street-address, or emergency details.

How quickly would someone respond?

For a real email inquiry, the expected response time is within 12 business hours during the preferred contact hours of Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time. The demonstration forms do not send anything, so they cannot receive a response.

Service area

Which communities does YYZ Caregivers serve?

Toronto's east end/Scarborough, North York, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, and Oshawa — generally within about 15 minutes' travel of YYZ Caregivers' home base.

Does the listed service area guarantee coverage at my address?

Not automatically, but nearby areas just outside this range are considered case by case — ask directly to confirm coverage at a specific address.

Pricing and scheduling

What does support cost?

Pricing is provided on request and is not published on this website. The initial in-home assessment visit is free.

What hours or availability are offered?

YYZ Caregivers is available Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time, with email as the preferred contact method. Visits can be cancelled or rescheduled without penalty; 48 hours' notice is appreciated where possible. The website does not offer live scheduling.

What qualifications or screening apply?

The founder is PSW-certified. Caregivers are screened through a vulnerable sector police check, a standard criminal record check, reference checks, and PSW credential verification. YYZ Caregivers carries active general liability insurance.

Privacy

Does the form send or store my information?

No. The pilot uses only temporary values on the current page. It makes no submission request, uses no database or browser storage, and clears the form after a successful preview.

Does the pilot use analytics or marketing cookies?

No. This build includes no analytics, marketing cookies, trackers, chat widgets, maps, remote fonts, or third-party form service.

Emergencies

Can I use this website for urgent help?

No. This website is not monitored for emergencies and does not provide emergency response. Seek appropriate local emergency assistance when immediate help is needed.

Still pending before public launch

A permanent logo, approved photography, a registered production domain, and a finalized privacy policy and terms of service are still pending. Service scope, screening, insurance, hours, and service area are confirmed as described above.
