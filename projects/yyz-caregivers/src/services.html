Proposed services

Everyday support, explained clearly

This pilot organizes possible non-clinical support into four categories. Each category and task must be confirmed by the owner before it can be offered publicly.

Jump to a proposed service

  • Personal routines
  • Companionship
  • Meals and home
  • Mobility-related help

Proposed category

Personal routines

Respectful assistance with parts of a daily routine may be considered after the exact service scope and worker training are confirmed.

May include

  • Getting ready for the day
  • Personal grooming routines
  • Dressing-related support
  • Evening routine assistance
Confirm with YYZ Caregivers

Proposed category

Companionship

Social connection may be shaped around the person's interests, preferred pace, and familiar routines.

May include

  • Conversation and shared activities
  • Accompaniment for local errands
  • Support with everyday planning
  • Connection during time at home
Confirm with YYZ Caregivers

Proposed category

Meals and home

Light help with meals and the home may support everyday routines without replacing regulated or specialized services.

May include

  • Simple meal preparation
  • Kitchen clean-up
  • Light household tasks
  • Organizing routine items
Confirm with YYZ Caregivers

Proposed category

Mobility-related help

Non-clinical assistance with everyday movement may be considered only within the assigned worker's confirmed training and permitted scope.

May include

  • Moving through familiar spaces
  • Getting ready for a local outing
  • Positioning everyday items within reach
  • Following an already-approved routine
Confirm with YYZ Caregivers
Ask about support needs

The pilot does not define clinical or emergency services

No category on this page confirms nursing, medical advice, medication management, emergency monitoring, therapy, guaranteed outcomes, or any task outside a worker's approved training and scope.

Exact exclusions, supervision, qualifications, and referral boundaries require owner and professional review.
