About YYZ Caregivers

A respectful approach starts with listening

YYZ Caregivers is an Ontario-registered, solo-operated private-home support service grounded in clear communication, personal choice, and everyday dignity.

Our mission

Make support easier to understand

The experience begins with plain information and a general conversation. It should help each person consider what feels useful while keeping their preferences and routines central.

YYZ Caregivers is a sole proprietorship operated directly by its founder, who is PSW-certified. A fuller founder story will be added here in a future update.

Our principles

Values that guide the experience

Dignity

Speak to adults with respect and preserve each person's agency in decisions about support.

Respect

Make room for individual routines, preferences, identities, homes, and family relationships.

Consistency

Present information and next steps predictably, from the first conversation onward.

Clear communication

Use plain language and avoid promises that have not been verified.

What respectful support means

The person receiving support remains at the centre

  • Ask about preferences rather than making assumptions.
  • Explain choices and boundaries in understandable language.
  • Support familiar routines without using fear or pressure.
  • Confirm the scope of every task before any service begins.

What's confirmed, and what's still pending

YYZ Caregivers is a registered Ontario sole proprietorship. The founder is PSW-certified, and caregivers are screened through a vulnerable sector police check, a standard criminal record check, reference checks, and PSW credential verification. YYZ Caregivers carries active general liability insurance.

Still pending before public launch: a permanent logo, approved photography, a registered production domain, a finalized privacy policy and terms of service, and a founder story for this page.
