About the pilot

A respectful approach starts with listening

YYZ Caregivers is being explored as a private-home support service grounded in clear communication, personal choice, and everyday dignity.

Intended mission

Make support easier to understand

The proposed experience begins with plain information and a general conversation. It should help each person consider what feels useful while keeping their preferences and routines central.

An owner-approved organization story, operating history, and team information have not yet been provided.

Pilot principles

Values intended to guide the experience

These principles describe the direction of this website, not verified claims about current business operations.

Dignity

Speak to adults with respect and preserve each person's agency in decisions about support.

Respect

Make room for individual routines, preferences, identities, homes, and family relationships.

Consistency

Present information and next steps predictably. Operational continuity remains to be confirmed.

Clear communication

Use plain language, identify uncertainties, and avoid promises that have not been verified.

What respectful support may mean

The person receiving support remains at the centre

  • Ask about preferences rather than making assumptions.
  • Explain choices and boundaries in understandable language.
  • Support familiar routines without using fear or pressure.
  • Confirm the scope of every task before any service begins.
