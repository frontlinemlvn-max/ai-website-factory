Pilot privacy information

What this local demonstration does with information

This page explains the actual behaviour of the static pilot. It is not legal advice or an approved privacy policy for a live service.

Pilot policy notice

A production privacy policy must be based on the owner's real operations, vendors, contact methods, retention practices, and legal review. Do not publish this draft as a final policy.

Information not collected

This static pilot does not operate a server, database, account system, booking system, or payment system. It does not ask for diagnosis, medication, treatment, health-card details, government identification, financial information, a street address, or a detailed care history.

Visitors should not enter health, medication, identification, financial, or emergency information anywhere in the pilot.

Demonstration form behaviour

The Contact and Support Needs pages use JavaScript in the current browser to check fields and show local results. They do not make a network request, send an email, create a booking, save a record, or place entries in browser storage.

The Contact form clears after a successful preview. The Support Needs tool keeps answers only while its page remains open so the visitor can review or edit them, and provides a clear control. Refreshing or leaving either page discards what was entered.

Cookies and analytics

The pilot sets no analytics or marketing cookies and includes no analytics code, advertising tracker, behavioural profiling, or consent-management platform.

A production analytics decision, if any, requires a documented purpose, consent approach where applicable, privacy review, and owner approval.

Third-party services

The pilot loads no remote fonts, maps, chat widgets, social media embeds, calendars, payment services, form processors, customer relationship tools, or other third-party runtime integrations.

The local preview server or browser may have its own technical behaviour outside these source files. No claim about a future hosting provider is made here.

Production review requirements

Before a live form or public website is approved, the owner must document what information is needed, why it is needed, who receives it, where it is processed, how long it is retained, who can access it, how it is deleted, which vendors are involved, and how incidents are handled.

The final policy must also identify the legal business, production contact details, applicable operating practices, and any owner-approved rights or request process. Appropriate professional review is required.
