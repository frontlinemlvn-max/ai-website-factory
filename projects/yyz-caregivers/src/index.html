A possible consultation path

Start with a general conversation

The production consultation process has not been approved. This pilot demonstrates a simple, low-pressure path.

  1. Share a general question

    Avoid health, medication, identification, financial, or emergency information.

  2. Discuss possible fit

    Future contact could clarify needs, service boundaries, and whether the proposed support may be suitable.

  3. Confirm next steps

    Any services, timing, pricing, and arrangements would need direct owner confirmation.
