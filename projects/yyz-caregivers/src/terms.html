Pilot-only use

This website is a local design and development pilot. It is provided only to review proposed information architecture, content, visual design, accessibility, and interactions.

It is not a live service channel and should not be used to arrange, purchase, schedule, or rely on personal support services.

No service or care relationship

Viewing the pilot, following its links, or completing the demonstration form does not create a client, care, advisory, employment, referral, or contractual relationship.

No service catalogue, worker assignment, consultation, care plan, price, schedule, or outcome is offered or agreed through this website.

No emergency use

The pilot is not monitored and does not provide emergency response, medical advice, nursing, crisis support, or urgent assistance. Seek appropriate local emergency assistance when immediate help is needed.

Accuracy and availability

Service categories, area references, process descriptions, brand choices, and other business information are provisional. They may change or be removed after owner review.

The pilot makes no commitment about service availability, response time, worker availability, qualifications, screening, insurance, price, operating hours, location coverage, or outcomes.

Intellectual property

Ownership, trade-name use, logo rights, photography rights, and final copyright language have not been documented for production. The text wordmark and abstract local artwork are pilot materials only.

External links

The pilot intentionally includes no external service, social media, booking, payment, map, or partner links. If approved links are added later, their destinations, purpose, accessibility, privacy implications, and opening behaviour must be reviewed.

Owner and professional review

Before launch, the owner must confirm the legal business identity, service terms, fees, cancellations, service boundaries, contact details, complaint process, privacy practices, accessibility process, content ownership, risk language, and governing terms.

Appropriate legal, privacy, insurance, employment, and service-scope review should be obtained for the real operating model.
